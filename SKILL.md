## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design does not draw the subject. A dense field of slightly shaky, imperfect horizontal dashes—dry marker or uneven screen-print—leaves the subject as one huge quiet white monument. One tiny whimsical naïve figure interacts with that negative shape. At most three colours: warm off-white paper, one muted bright ink taken from the source, and black. Flat graphic only, with analog print grain. It is not a realistic drawing, a gradient, a shadow, a smooth digital render, or a busy multi-colour layout.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
